Automated Plant Irrigation System

Arduino soil-moisture controller that switches a relay-driven pump on calibrated thresholds to water plants without overwatering.

Role
Designer & builder
Where
Wentworth Institute of Technology
When
Apr 2024

Overview

A design project to find an everyday problem and build a fix: houseplants that die because nobody remembers to water them.

An Arduino continuously reads a soil-moisture sensor, compares it against calibrated thresholds, and switches a pump through a relay — on when the soil is too dry, off once it's back at a healthy level.

What I did

  • Wired and assembled the sensor, relay, pump, tubing and microcontroller.
  • Wrote and calibrated the threshold control logic.
  • Tested repeatedly in real soil; the prototype delivered consistent watering without overwatering.
  • Next steps identified: a proper enclosure, a single integrated unit and drip irrigation.
