PicoSatellite — Power & Radio Hardware

Power budgeting and radio-board PCB redesign for a student-built 5 cm × 5 cm satellite that will image Earth's horizon.

Role
Electrical / Power Subsystem
Where
Wentworth PicoSatellite Team
When
Sep 2024 – Present

Overview

A student team of nearly twenty engineers is building a 5 cm × 5 cm picosatellite for Wentworth Institute of Technology. Its mission is to photograph Earth's horizon in support of weather-monitoring work, and it has to do that inside very tight limits on mass, volume and power.

The satellite is split into power, structures, communications, payload and software subsystems. I work on the electrical side: proving the power system can keep the mission alive, and building the hardware that flies.

What I did

  • Built a state-based power model covering idle, processing, radio transmission and camera-capture phases to check that the battery, solar charging and power distribution can sustain every mode.
  • Pulled electrical characteristics from every component datasheet and documented them as the single source for the power budget.
  • Taught myself KiCad and redesigned a subsystem PCB for updated components and cleaner routing.
  • Sourced parts for the radio board and did fine-pitch soldering and assembly for test.
