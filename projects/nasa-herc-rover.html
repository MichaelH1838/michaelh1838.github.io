NASA HERC 2027 Rover — Electrical Lead

Sole electrical engineer on Wentworth's remote-controlled rover for NASA's Human Exploration Rover Challenge: power, safety and control architecture.

Role
Electrical Lead
Where
Wentworth HERC Team (RC Division)
When
2026 – Present

Overview

Wentworth is entering NASA's Human Exploration Rover Challenge 2027 in the College Remote-Controlled division. The rover is a six-wheel rocker-bogie with four-corner Ackermann steering. I'm the only electrical engineer on a team of mechanical engineers, so the full electrical system is mine.

Low-level drive and steering run on an Arduino Mega 2560. Rev 2.0 adds a Raspberry Pi 5 with a NoIR camera, LiDAR ranging and an RTL-SDR receiver for APRS commands.

What I did

  • Designed the power architecture: 3S LiPo, 40 A main fuse, branch fuses for drive motors, a 6 V servo buck and avionics.
  • Built the safety chain around a normally-closed mushroom E-stop that drives the main relay coil.
  • Capturing the full system schematic in KiCad with a custom symbol library.
  • Giving the electrical input for the team's hazard analysis and FMEA.
