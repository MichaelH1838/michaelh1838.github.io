Analog Temperature Sensor Front-End + PCB

LM335 + OP07 signal-conditioning chain that maps 0–100 °C onto the full Arduino ADC range, calibrated with water-bath tests and finished as a custom PCB.

Role
Designer — circuit, calibration & PCB
Where
Wentworth Institute of Technology
When
Oct 2025

Overview

The LM335 only swings about 2.73 V to 3.73 V across 0–100 °C, which wastes most of a 0–5 V ADC. I designed an OP07-based analog interface with adjustable gain and offset that stretches that 1 V window across the converter's full range.

The final readout reports temperature in Celsius and Fahrenheit.

What I did

  • Chose the amplifier topology, calculated every resistor value and drew the schematic, adding trim pots for calibration.
  • Measured the Arduino's real 5 V reference instead of trusting the nominal value.
  • Mapped the LM335's voltage-to-temperature curve with water-bath tests, then recalculated gain and offset and recalibrated on the breadboard.
  • After validation, designed a custom PCB for the full sensor and signal-conditioning system, ready for fabrication.
