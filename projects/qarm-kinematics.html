Q-Arm Kinematics & Pick-and-Place

Forward/inverse kinematics and motion planning in Python for a 4-DOF Quanser Q-Arm, with RGB-D vision checks and current-sensed gripping.

Role
Lead Python developer
Where
Wentworth Institute of Technology
When

Overview

Control software for the Quanser Q-Arm, a 4-degree-of-freedom serial manipulator, taking it from kinematic equations to repeatable pick-and-place on real hardware.

What I did

  • Modeled the link transforms with Denavit-Hartenberg parameters and wrote an IK solver that converts target (x, y, z) positions into base, shoulder, elbow and wrist angles.
  • Wrote motion planning for smooth, collision-free trajectories and control of the two-stage tendon gripper.
  • Used gripper current sensing to estimate grip force and avoid mechanical strain.
  • Used the onboard Intel RealSense RGB-D camera to confirm alignment with targets in real time.
  • Ran multi-step sorting and placement routines with high repeatability.
