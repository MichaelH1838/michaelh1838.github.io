Analog Active Noise-Cancellation Prototype

Electret mic → biased pre-amp → 741 inverting stage that phase-flips ambient noise for destructive interference at the headphones.

Role
Designer, builder & debugger
Where
Wentworth Institute of Technology
When
Dec 2025

Overview

Noise cancellation with no DSP at all: pick up ambient noise, flip its phase, and add it back so it cancels at the ear.

An electret microphone feeds a custom front end with biasing and a pre-amplification network for clean gain. A 741 op-amp configured as an inverting amplifier then produces the anti-phase signal, and tuning its gain against the external audio source produces destructive interference at the headphone output.

What I did

  • Designed and verified the full circuit in Multisim before building.
  • Built the pre-amp and inverting stages on a breadboard.
  • Tested and debugged with a multimeter and oscilloscope, tuning gain until perceived noise dropped.
