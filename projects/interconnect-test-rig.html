Interconnect Test Rig for BMS Boards

A production test fixture that validates six battery-management-system PCBs at once under DC and AC stimulus, with live voltage readout.

Role
Lead designer & debugger
Where
Electroswitch · Weymouth, MA
When
Feb 2026

Overview

Testing battery-monitoring boards one at a time was slow. I designed and debugged an interconnect test fixture that checks six BMS PCBs in parallel across a range of operating conditions.

The rig shows real-time voltage levels while the boards are driven with DC and AC input signals, so a technician can see a failing channel immediately instead of probing board by board.

What I did

  • Led the design of the fixture's interconnect so six boards share stimulus and measurement.
  • Defined the DC and AC test conditions that cover each board's operating range.
  • Brought the rig up and debugged it until readings were consistent across all six positions.
